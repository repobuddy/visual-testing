Goal: find out what changed when a story snapshot fails.
The general reading order is in [Reviewing failures](/visual-testing/visual-testing/reviewing-failures/).
This page covers what is specific to the addon.

## Three places to look

1. **The test error.** `vitest run` prints the mismatch with the pixel count, the options, the three image paths, the changed regions and the review image path. [Reviewing failures with agents](/visual-testing/visual-testing/reviewing-failures-with-agents/) shows a full message.
2. **The Vis panel.** Run Storybook in development mode, open the failed story, and use the [Vis panel](/visual-testing/addon/guides/the-vis-panel/) to swipe, blend or diff the baseline and the result.
3. **The files.** The baseline, result and diff sit in `__baselines__`, `__results__` and `__diffs__` under the snapshot root.

## The review image

By default a mismatch also writes `<name>.review.png` and `<name>.regions.json` next to the diff.
The review image places the baseline, the result and the diff of each changed region side by side.
When the image size changed, there are many regions, or the regions cover most of the image,
it places the whole baseline, result and diff side by side instead.
Set `reviewImage: 'full'` to always get the whole-image version.
The regions file is written either way.
Turn the review image off with `reviewImage: false` on `storybookVis()`.
The option comes from the plugin, see the [configuration reference](/visual-testing/plugin/configuration/).

The Vis panel does not show the review image.
Open it from disk, or give it to an agent.

## Stories that fail only in CI

A baseline is per platform, so a story can pass on your machine and fail on CI.
See [CI](/visual-testing/addon/guides/ci/).
